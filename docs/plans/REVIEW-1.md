Visible-frame verdict; features may exist outside these crops.

### A. Reserve

- Right: overall base footprint feels credible—rail yard/White Rook, K-building grid, storage bunkers, tarmac, chess-building cluster, perimeter roads and southern cliffs form a recognizable Reserve.
- Train Station beside White Rook is plausible, though the two labels need clearer anchors.
- Black Pawn is conspicuously missing; White Pawn appears detached from the expected helipad-side pawn/bishop cluster.
- Helipad/helicopter—the map’s strongest central orientation landmark—is neither clearly modeled nor labelled.
- Dome/White Queen is absent from these views; players will immediately miss its silhouette and relationship to the cliffs.
- “Barracks” is too generic compared with established chess-piece callouts.
- “Bunkers” is inadequate: Bunker Hermetic Door, Depot Hermetic Door, D-2/command bunker and pawn/bishop tunnel connections need explicit treatment.
- No obvious gross inversion among the visible Knights/Bishops, but identical building treatment and colliding labels make their exact placement hard to verify. Those landmarks are core Reserve navigation references. ([Reserve topology reference](https://odealo.com/articles/escape-from-tarkov-reserve-map-beginners-guide))

### B. Woods

- Right: macro layout reads well—lake and Sawmill, USEC area northwest, pond village north, Scav Town by the river, Old Sawmill east, Military Camp southeast, and Crash Site/Jaeger corridor are plausibly related.
- Sawmill’s sheds, log paths and adjacent road are recognizable in 3D.
- “USED CAMP” is a visible typo; it must be “USEC CAMP.”
- “Cultist Village” is understandable, but “Sunken Village / Abandoned Village” is the more useful primary callout.
- Scav House is missing or not prominent enough, despite being a major western/southwestern orientation landmark.
- River bridges need names and stronger endpoints: Bridge V-Ex, Scav Bridge and Railway Bridge matter more than several minor labels.
- Rocks/cliffs are broadly placed plausibly, but the 3D forms are too monolithic; Sniper Rock, mountain spine and navigable gaps do not read distinctly.
- The tight 3D Sawmill view loses the lake/shore relationship, while the hill view has almost no identifying context. ([current Woods landmark set](https://www.tarkov.dev/map/woods?q=591afe0186f77431bd616a11))

### C. Cross-map

- Sidebar, typography, muted palette, green selection accents and control placement appear consistent with the Customs design language.
- 2D satellite views have much stronger hierarchy and navigational value than the current 3D views.
- Large labels collide with markers and one another; several are clipped at viewport edges.
- Markers stack into unreadable piles and appear to scale excessively with zoom.
- Minor labels become illegibly small on satellite imagery, while major 3D labels become overwhelmingly large.
- 3D roads, buildings and cliffs have insufficient edge/tonal separation.
- Add an unmistakable north/rotation indicator; Reserve’s competing community orientations make this essential.
- “Floors” appearing only in 3D is sensible, but Reserve needs an obvious underground-level affordance.

### D. Top 8 fixes

1. Correct and complete Reserve’s chess-piece anchors, especially Black Pawn and White Pawn around the helipad.
2. Add unmistakable Reserve helicopter/helipad and Dome/White Queen landmarks.
3. Provide a real Reserve underground layer with D-2, command bunker and hermetic-door connections.
4. Fix “USED CAMP” to “USEC CAMP” and promote Scav House on Woods.
5. Add Woods’ named bridges and clearer extract-side road endpoints.
6. Implement zoom-based label priority, collision avoidance and edge-safe placement.
7. Keep marker icons screen-sized and cluster overlapping markers with counts.
8. Improve 3D LOD/antialiasing and landmark differentiation, especially cliffs, rocks and building silhouettes.
